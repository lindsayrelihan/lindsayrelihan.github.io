I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at the Mitch Daniels School of Business at Purdue University. I am an applied microeconomist with interests in urban economics, household finance, and real estate. My research focuses on how new technologies reshape the spatial relationships between firms and consumers, and their impact on cities. I received my PhD in Applied Economics from The Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania in 2018.
